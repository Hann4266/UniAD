\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Notes:}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
  \item \textbf{nuScenes baseline}: Spacial+AA (BEV spatial + Agent-Agent attention, TrackFormer perception input, no Agent-Map interaction). Chosen as the fair comparison for LOKI, which has no HD map input. Uses 6 surround cameras + LiDAR depth supervision.
  \item \textbf{Zero-shot}: nuScenes-trained weights evaluated directly on LOKI (10 nuScenes classes mapped to 8 LOKI classes).
  \item \textbf{Fine-tune}: nuScenes pre-trained backbone, fine-tuned on LOKI for 6 epochs.
  \item \textbf{Pre-FOV filter}: Trained before FOV and camera-visibility filtering were implemented; GT includes invisible objects.
  \item \textbf{Stride}: \texttt{queue\_stride} (temporal frame spacing). Default = 1; stride = 4 corresponds to 0.8\,s spacing at 5\,FPS.
  \item \textbf{Intent-filtered}: Trained on 367/515 scenes that contain $\geq$1 rare intent (LCL/LCR/TL/TR).
  \item \textbf{Stage 2 mAP/AMOTA}: From the frozen perception backbone at eval time; may differ from Stage 1 due to epoch selection.
  \item All LOKI experiments: single front camera (60\textdegree{} FOV), no LiDAR, no HD maps
\end{itemize}
