\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Notes:}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
  \item \textbf{nuScenes baseline}: Spacial+AA (BEV spatial + Agent-Agent attention, TrackFormer perception input, no Agent-Map interaction). Chosen as the fair comparison for LOKI, which has no HD map input. Uses 6 surround cameras + LiDAR depth supervision.
  \item \textbf{Zero-shot}: nuScenes-trained weights evaluated directly on LOKI (10 nuScenes classes mapped to 8 LOKI classes).
  \item \textbf{Fine-tune}: nuScenes pre-trained backbone, fine-tuned on LOKI for 6 epochs.
  \item \textbf{GT Filt.}: Visibility/imbalance filtering applied to GT during training and eval. \texttt{N} = no filtering (training sees all 360\textdegree{} agents including those outside the 60\textdegree{} camera FOV and those occluded). \texttt{Y} = FOV filter (removes $\sim$57\% behind/beside the ego) + camera-visibility filter (removes $\sim$46\% of the remaining without a 2D bbox). \texttt{Y+L} = adds LiDAR\,$\geq$\,1 filter on top (drops a further $\sim$5\% with zero LiDAR returns). Verified by inspecting the pipeline dumped in each run's training log (\texttt{ObjectFOVFilterTrack} + \texttt{ObjectCameraVisibleFilter} present).
  \item \textbf{Stride}: \texttt{queue\_stride} (temporal frame spacing). Default = 1; stride = 4 corresponds to 0.8\,s spacing at 5\,FPS.
  \item \textbf{Intent-filtered}: Trained on 367/515 scenes that contain $\geq$1 rare intent (LCL/LCR/TL/TR).
  \item \textbf{Fixed evaluator}: All LOKI Stage 1 and Stage 2 rows have been scored on the current 4236-frame LOKI val pkl with active-class averaging and camera-visible GT filtering. S1.2 and S1.6 predictions were generated by Kubernetes GPU jobs and re-scored from saved pkls after the distributed evaluator segfaulted post-write. Stage 2 rows were re-scored from saved \texttt{results\_epoch20.pkl} files (no re-inference / re-training needed: frozen perception backbone + eval-only fix). Intent metrics were already correctly camera-visibility filtered, so Acc and F1 are essentially unchanged from the prior numbers; mAP and AMOTA shifted upward as the eval denominator was tightened.
  \item \textbf{Stage 2 mAP/AMOTA}: From the frozen perception backbone at eval time; may differ from Stage 1 due to epoch selection.
  \item All LOKI experiments: single front camera (60\textdegree{} FOV), no LiDAR, no HD maps
\end{itemize}
